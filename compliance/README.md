# Compliance

This directory maps architecture concerns and controls to external laws, standards, and risk-management frameworks.

## Purpose

Compliance material helps architects understand how external obligations influence system design, governance, evidence, and operating responsibilities. It does not replace legal advice or authoritative source material.

## Planned Areas

- EU AI Act mapping;
- Article 50 transparency;
- AI risk classification;
- evidence catalogue.

Additional frameworks may be added when they materially improve the architecture, such as ISO/IEC 42001 or the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

## Content Rules

- Cite authoritative sources.
- Distinguish legal requirements from recommendations and architecture interpretation.
- State scope, assumptions, effective dates, and uncertainty.
- Map obligations to controls and evidence rather than reproducing legislation.
- Revalidate time-sensitive content when it changes.

## Boundaries

This directory explains external constraints. Control definitions belong in [`../controls/`](../controls/), architecture structures in [`../reference-architecture/`](../reference-architecture/), and implementation patterns in [`../patterns/`](../patterns/).
